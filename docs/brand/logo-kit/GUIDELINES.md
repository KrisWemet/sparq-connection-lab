# Sparq — Logo Guidelines

## 1. The logo

**The idea:** two halves of one spark, split by an S. The spark only exists when both halves show up. It's the
S of Sparq, and the "spark" in the name, without hearts, rings or silhouettes.

**The family:** "Sparq" is the family name. The line underneath names the app, and the way the spark is split says
who the app is for:

| App | Mark | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Sparq Connection | split in two by the S | the two of you |
| Sparq Within | whole, with a small spark inside | just you |
| Sparq Family | split four ways | all of you |

**Versions** (each in colour, plum, ink, white and reversed):
- **Horizontal lockup**: the primary logo. Use it wherever there's room.
- **Stacked lockup**: square-ish spaces such as splash screens, posters and social images.
- **Symbol**: the app icon, avatars, and places where the name is already nearby.
- **Small spark**: the plain spark with no split. Use it for anything shown below 32 px, such as favicons and
  notification badges.
- **Wordmark**: "Sparq" alone, for the family brand when no single app is meant.

## 2. Clear space

Keep a clear zone around the logo the size of the **capital S in "Sparq"** on every side. Nothing else (text,
edges, other logos) goes inside it. The zone scales with the logo; never use a fixed distance.

## 3. Minimum size

| Version | Screen | Print |
|---|---|---|
| Horizontal lockup | 220 px wide | 50 mm wide |
| Stacked lockup | 140 px wide | 32 mm wide |
| Symbol (split) | 32 px | 8 mm |
| Small spark | 16 px | 4 mm |

Below 32 px, always switch from the split symbol to the small spark.

## 4. Colour

| Name | Use | HEX | RGB | CMYK (approx.) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Plum | wordmark, lower-right half, app-icon tile | `#4B2E57` | 75 46 87 | 14 47 0 66 |
| Coral | upper-left half | `#E97868` | 233 120 104 | 0 48 55 9 |
| Linen | light background, reversed half | `#FAF7F2` | 250 247 242 | 0 1 3 2 |
| Ink | one-colour version | `#241D27` | 36 29 39 | 8 26 0 85 |
| Gold | reserved for the app (growth moments), not used in the logo | `#F3B55A` | 243 181 90 | 0 26 63 5 |

The CMYK values are straight conversions. Before printing, match them by eye with your printer, using a Pantone guide.

**Approved logo and background pairs:**
- **Colour logo:** on linen or white.
- **Reversed logo** (coral + linen, linen wordmark): on plum.
- **White logo:** on ink or on dark photos.
- **Ink or plum one-colour logo:** on white or linen.

On photos, put the logo on a calm area, or place it on a plum or linen panel.

## 5. Typography

- **Wordmark:** "Sparq" is Cormorant Garamond Bold, converted to outlines. Product names are Inter Medium in
  capitals, spaced 0.44 em apart.
- **The app:** Cormorant Garamond for headings and Inter for text, the same families as the logo.
- **Licence:** both fonts use the SIL Open Font License, which allows logo use. Never retype the wordmark; always
  use the files.

**Tagline:** "Stronger individuals create stronger relationships". Set it in Cormorant Garamond Medium Italic, in
sentence case with no full stop, centred under the lockup with a short coral rule between them. The tagline can go
with the logo but is never part of it, so keep it off the app icon and anything under 400 px wide.

## 6. Don'ts

- **Don't distort it.** No stretching, squashing or rotating. The spark is always upright.
- **Don't recolour it** outside the palette, and don't swap coral and plum between the halves.
- **Don't add effects** such as shadows, outlines, gradients or glows.
- **Don't change the parts:**
  - Don't change the gap in the spark, or fill it in, except in the small spark.
  - Don't rearrange, resize or re-space the parts of a lockup.
  - Don't use the split symbol below 32 px.
- **Don't place it on busy backgrounds** without a panel behind it.
