# SPARQ_VISION.md

> "Stronger individuals create stronger relationships. Stronger relationships create stronger families. Stronger families create stronger communities."

A short vision brief. The governing document is `docs/CONSTITUTION.md` (v1.2); where this brief and the constitution differ, the constitution wins.

## Core Philosophy
- **A guided transformation system:** Sparq helps people discover who they want to become, understand why it matters, and practice becoming that person in the real world — starting with their relationship.
- **Individual First:** We focus on the *user's* own behaviors and thoughts, never on fixing their partner.
- **Sparq leads the path; the user chooses the destination.** Peter guides, challenges and structures practice; the user decides what they value, who they want to be, and every major life decision.
- **Adaptive & Relational:** The app feels like a warm guide (Peter the Otter), not a clinician. It learns quietly through natural conversation — and every guess is a hypothesis the user can see and correct.
- **Goal:** 2,500 active paying users in Year 1 — earned by real change in users' lives, not time in app.

## The First 14 Days
- **No Clinical Quizzes:** We never ask "What is your attachment style?"
- **Quiet, transparent learning:** Peter learns from daily reflections; his guesses live on the user's Insight Profile, never as labels.
- **Daily Loop** (the daily form of the Transformation Engine):
  1. **Morning Story:** A relatable scenario from a rotating cast, teaching a concept without jargon.
  2. **Daily Action:** A small real-world mission the user chooses (or makes their own), ideally tied to a real moment ("when I get home…").
  3. **Evening Reflection:** What actually happened — setbacks are information, never failure.

## The Skill Tree (Day 15+)
- **Progressive depth:** Users explore tracks (Communication, Conflict, Intimacy) at Basic → Advanced → Expert, stepping up when their own practice shows a level has become easy.
- **Monetization:** Upgrades are described plainly as more depth — never scarcity, urgency, or upsells tied to streaks. Safety tools are always free.

## The Voice (Peter the Otter)
- **Tone:** Warm, 4th-grade reading level, non-clinical. Never claims human feelings.
- **Feedback Style:**
  - **Baseline:** Celebration of effort, pointing to real evidence and letting the user say what it means.
  - **Adaptive:** Comfort and stabilization when the user is overwhelmed; gentle, one-time challenge when they're ready; direct ideas when they ask.

## Key Features
- **The Translator:** Helps a user rephrase their own message, using only what *they* type about their partner — never the partner's private data or labels.
- **Conflict First Aid:** A calm-down path for real-time de-escalation, always free.
- **"Us":** A shared space with only what each partner explicitly chooses to share.
